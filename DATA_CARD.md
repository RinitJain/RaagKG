## Fields (`data/raagkg.csv`)

List fields are separated by `; ` in the CSV and are arrays in the JSON.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `raga` | Raga name in common romanised spelling |
| `thaat` | Parent scale, one of Bhatkhande's ten thaats |
| `aroha` | Ascending line |
| `avaroha` | Descending line |
| `vadi` | Most important note (two values joined by "or" where sources differ) |
| `samvadi` | Second most important note |
| `pakad` | Signature phrase |
| `jaati` | Number of notes in ascent and descent (Audav 5, Shadav 6, Sampurna 7) |
| `prahar` | Time of performance, as written by the curators |
| `prahar_slot` | That time mapped to one of eight three-hour watches (1 = 6 AM to 9 AM ... 8 = 3 AM to 6 AM), or `any` |
| `rasa` | Mood(s) the raga evokes, e.g. Shringar, Karuna, Bhakti |
| `prakriti` | Temperament, as descriptive words, e.g. Romantic, Pensive |
| `ritu` | Season: spring, summer, monsoon, autumn, winter, `all` (any season), or empty when no season is recorded |
| `samaprakritic` | Related ragas sharing much of the same material, resolved to catalogue names |
| `samaprakritic_unresolved` | Related ragas named in the sheet that are not in the catalogue |

## Graph

Node labels: `Raga`, `Thaat`, `Prahar`, `Rasa`, `Prakriti`, `Ritu`, `Jaati`,
`Swar`. Aroha, avaroha and pakad are stored as properties of the raga node.
Relationships, all from a raga: `belongs_to_thaat`, `performed_in`,
`has_rasa`, `has_prakriti`, `associated_with_season`, `has_jaati`,
`vadi_swar`, `samvadi_swar`, `samaprakritic` (raga to raga).

## How it was made

The authors compiled every attribute by hand from V. N. Bhatkhande's
*Kramik Pustak Malika* and V. D. Paluskar's *Sangeet Bal Prakash*, and
checked them against three public references: SwarGanga, Raga Junglism and Artium Academy. No
attribute was generated by a language model.

`prahar_slot` and the normalised `ritu` were derived by
`scripts/build_release.py`. A time range that spans two watches is assigned to
the watch that holds most of it (11 PM to 2 AM becomes watch 7). The mapping
for every time written in the sheet is listed in that script.

## Corrections

Corrections to the source sheet are listed in `CHANGELOG.md`; the sheet
itself is kept unchanged in `data/raw/`.

## Known gaps

- Miyan Ki Malhar has no temperament recorded.
- "Bilawal", listed as related to Alhaiya Bilawal, is not linked, because it
  could mean Shuddha Bilawal or Alhaiya Bilawal itself.
- `prakriti` is free description, so near-synonyms appear as separate values
  (69 in all).
- An apostrophe after a note means the upper octave for S, R and G, but in a
  few phrases (for example `N' R G` in Yaman) it marks the lower octave. The
  meaning is clear from the phrase; the notation is kept as the curators wrote
  it.
- Kedar's lines write Ma as a plain `M`; elsewhere the catalogue uses `m` for
  natural Ma and `M'` for sharp Ma.
- `reports/scale_check.csv` lists ragas whose notes fall outside their thaat.
  Most of these are borrowed notes that the raga genuinely uses (Bihag's
  sharp Ma, for instance). They are listed for review, not as errors.

## Intended use

Research on music information retrieval, knowledge graphs and grounded
generation; teaching aids; and checking or constraining what a music
generation system produces. Raga practice varies between gharanas and
teachers, so the catalogue records one well-sourced reading of each raga, not
the only correct one.
